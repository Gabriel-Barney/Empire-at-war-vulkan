## Summary

- 24 of 24 `_MP_LAND_*` maps place relevant points (69 placements in all).
- CONTROL-tagged maps (18): BESPIN, BOTHAWUI, CORELLIA, DAGOBAH, DANTOOINE, ENDOR, FELUCIA, GEONOSIS, HONOGHR, HOTH, HYPORI, KAMINO, KESSEL, MANDALORE, NABOO, SALEUCAMI, TATOOINE, YAVIN.
- Faction-fixed `Reinforcement_Point_<Faction>*` placed directly in the map files: 0. They do appear in game: 24 maps carry 51 `Team_NN_Reinforcement_Point_Plus2_Cap` team markers, and the skirmish setup replaces each by `Reinforcement_Point_<slot NN faction>` owned by slot NN (retail run 20261108-214851 on Kamino: Lua saw `Reinforcement_Point_Rebel` owned by Rebel and `Reinforcement_Point_Empire` owned by Empire next to the two starts; port: tactical_battle_bt.cpp team_pad). They have no CAPTURE_POINT and no relevant flag, so they never count for 0x15.
- Relevant points with a raw owner other than Neutral (3): none.
- So, if the 2.1 remap holds, every relevant point on every map starts **Neutral** (the [M] of SKIRMISH_SETUP 4.2), except the ones listed in the line above. The in-game run checks the start owner on the probe map from memory (reader `POINT` lines at the first land frame) and from Lua (`PT` records).
- Not answerable offline: whether the engine changes a point owner between the remap and frame 0, and the owner of a non-Neutral raw point when its faction plays; the probe run covers the first for its map.
